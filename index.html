Principles Behind Every System

Clear modules and services, so features can grow or change without breaking the rest of the system.

Careful schema design, migrations and relational modeling keep data consistent, from ledgers to patient records.

Case studies

Case study CountComply · RemitCore internship, 2026

Inside CountComply: compliance in one place

Problem
Compliance teams juggle screening, policies, risks, reminders and filings across spreadsheets and email, which makes deadlines easy to miss and audits painful.
Approach
One platform for AI-assisted AML/KYC screening, policies, enterprise risks, obligations, filing tracking, board oversight and a full audit trail, on an event-driven Java Vert.x backend with a Vue.js interface and PostgreSQL.
My role
System development and system design, plus testing, as part of the RemitCore development team. Before that, I worked on the compliance and data analysis side in 2025 (Excel, Power BI, pandas), which shaped how I designed the product.
See it live at countcomply.com
Case study BioPay · Humanitarian payments

Inside BioPay: identity you can pay against

Problem
Humanitarian programmes need to register people, confirm each person is who they say they are, and then pay them or issue vouchers, often where the internet is unreliable.
Approach
An offline-first platform for biometric registration, payments and vouchers. Liveness detection runs on the ONNX runtime, and biometric payloads are processed with low latency so a registration or payment does not keep people waiting.
My role
I worked on the whole platform: the frontend, the backend and the app, and deployed it to production on a custom domain (biopay.africa).
See it live at biopay.africa
